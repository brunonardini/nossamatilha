# Business information

Source of truth for site content. Extracted from the owner's boarding brochure (`tmp/marca/Hospedagem_Nossa_Matilha_corrigido.pdf`). Quoted terms are the pt-BR wording used by the business — reuse them in site copy.

> The brochure lists the old domain (`nossamatilhadogs.com.br`). The current site is **https://www.nossamatilharesende.com.br/**.

## Location and hours

- City: Resende/RJ.
- Business hours: 7h–18h (check-in and pick-up only in this window).
- Bookings, quotes, cancellations and written instructions go through the "WhatsApp oficial da Nossa Matilha".

Contact channels are not recorded here because this repository is public: phone numbers come from environment variables ([environment.md](environment.md)) and the Instagram URL lives in `lib/contact.ts`. Ask the owner before adding any other contact data (see "Privacy and LGPD" in `AGENTS.md`).

## Reputation

- **Google rating** (Google Maps business profile): varies between 4,8 and 5,0, always shown as five stars. The home hero seal shows only the five stars (no label), never the number (owner's decision).
- **FBAA certification**: the lead trainer is certified by FBAA (see Team); the seal shows "Certificação FBAA".

## People

- **Silvia Matrumoto** — CEO, public face of the brand, and the lead trainer certified by FBAA. Usually wears the team uniform (white shirt with green sleeves and brown trim).
- **Bacon** — Silvia's dog, a young male yellow Labrador Retriever with a lean build, caramel coat, white chest patch and a blue collar. He is the narrator of the site.

## Services

- **Hospedagem** (boarding) — main service described in the brochure.
- **Creche** (daycare) — dogs spend the day in the daycare areas; staff trained as daycare monitors.
- **Banho e tosa** (bath and grooming) — provided on-site by partner **Encantacão**, booked directly with the partner on their WhatsApp (`NEXT_PUBLIC_GROOMING_WHATSAPP_PHONE_NUMBER`). Can be added to a boarding stay so the dog goes home clean.

## About ("Quem somos")

"Um espaço completo de lazer, cuidado e descanso para o seu cão."

- **Monitoramento 24h** — qualified staff supervise the dogs full time.
- **Socialização natural** — dogs are grouped by energy level, not size.
- **Rotina do dia** — supervised activities, attention to each dog's needs.
- **Noite tranquila** — dogs rest in a cozy room with an overnight attendant (plantonista / vigia noturno).

## Space ("Nosso espaço")

More than 1,200 m² for leisure, recreation and rest (confirmed by the owner; older brochure material says 1,100 m², which is outdated). Capacity: up to 32 dogs.

## Team ("Nossa equipe")

- Lead trainer: Silvia Matrumoto, certified by FBAA (Federação Brasileira dos Adestradores de Animais).
- Continuous training for the whole team.
- Everyone caring for boarded dogs is trained as a veterinary assistant (auxiliar veterinário).
- Staff trained as dog daycare monitors (monitor de creche).

## Requirements ("Vacinas obrigatórias")

- V10 (multiple polyvalent vaccine) — required.
- Rabies (Raiva) — required.
- Flu (Gripe) — recommended.
- Antiparasitic and dewormer up to date.
- Updated vaccination card: send a photo together with the registration form.

Not accepted: dogs with worm symptoms, flea or tick infestation, or females in heat.

## Booking ("Como reservar")

Reservation only, requested via the official WhatsApp, at least **48 hours** before check-in, subject to availability. Send:

1. Completed registration form (ficha cadastral).
2. Photo of the updated vaccination card.
3. Expected check-in and check-out dates and times.
4. Payment receipt.

The booking is confirmed only after documents are checked, availability is confirmed, rules are accepted and payment is made. A quote is not a booking. Packages also require 48 hours' notice.

> **Personal data (LGPD).** The registration form, vaccination card, veterinary prescriptions, payment receipts and feeding/health instructions contain personal data about the dog's owner and sensitive information about the dog. They are exchanged **only through the official WhatsApp**. The site must never collect, upload, store or log them: booking and quote calls to action link to WhatsApp, and any future site form follows the data-minimization rules in "Privacy and LGPD" (`AGENTS.md`).

## Pricing ("Valores")

- **R$ 120,00 per day (diária)**.
- A diária is 24 hours counted from check-in; after that a new diária is charged.
- No half-days: stays shorter than 24 hours (including same-day in and out) are charged as a full diária.
- Earlier bookings may get special conditions or discounts — not automatic, stated in the quote. Quotes via WhatsApp with dates and times.
- Promotions, when available, are announced on social media.

## Cancellation

Requested via the official WhatsApp. Notice is counted from the scheduled check-in time to the time the message was sent.

| Notice                     | Option A (refund)         | Option B (credit)                  |
| -------------------------- | ------------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| 48 h or more               | 90% refund (10% retained) | 100% as credit, valid for 3 months |
| Less than 48 h, or no-show | 60% refund (40% retained) | 90% as credit, valid for 3 months  |

Options are alternatives; retentions do not add up. Credits are valid for 3 months from the cancellation date, must be booked in advance subject to availability, and the new stay is priced at the rate in effect then (difference paid by the owner).

**Extending a stay** requires a request to Nossa Matilha, subject to availability and team confirmation. Staying beyond the contracted period may be charged as a new diária.

## What to bring ("O que trazer")

- Required: enough food for the whole stay, plus medication if any.
- Optional: personal items (bed, blanket, toys). Beds and toys are already provided.
- Do not bring food and water bowls: the business uses its own, which are all sterilizable and suited for use on site.
- Feeding amounts and times must be given in writing (note in the dog's bag or via WhatsApp).
- Dietary restrictions, allergies, health conditions and special care must be written in the registration form or sent via WhatsApp before check-in.

## Medication

- Must come with a veterinary prescription (delivered with the dog's items or as a photo via WhatsApp).
- Instructions must state medication name, dose, times and treatment duration.
- **All instructions must be in writing** — never only verbally, because of staff shift changes.

## Daily routine ("Nossa rotina")

- **Morning**: dogs go out to the yard; breakfast served individually, one dog at a time, supervised; then play and socialization in the daycare areas. Lunch break for dogs that need or are used to a midday meal.
- **Afternoon**: play or rest according to each dog's profile. Gate service only during business hours; after closing the team focuses on the boarded dogs.
- **Night**: dinner served individually; recreational activities; then dogs go to the rest rooms and spend the night with an overnight attendant.
